# Resources

This resource page compiles high-quality, freely available online materials for learning **Classical Chinese** (文言文). Resources are organized by practical learning categories to support progressive skill development: building vocabulary, mastering grammar, engaging in extensive reading, practicing composition, and understanding cultural/historical context.

## Vocabulary

- [Chinese Text Project Dictionary](https://ctext.org/dictionary.pl?if=en) - An integrated dictionary drawing directly from classical sources, offering definitions, early text occurrences, and character radical breakdowns in context.
- [Zdic / 汉典](https://www.zdic.net/) - A comprehensive Chinese character and classical dictionary featuring Kangxi Dictionary (*Kangxi Zidian*) definitions, *Shuowen Jiezi* etymologies, variant forms, and historical phonology.
- [Chinese Notes Dictionary & Digital Library](https://chinesenotes.com/) - A dedicated digital lexicon and corpus reader for Literary Chinese featuring English equivalents, part-of-speech categorization, and word-by-word glosses.
- [Wikiversity Classical Chinese Vocabulary](https://en.wikiversity.org/wiki/Classical_Chinese/Vocabulary) - A structured vocabulary list covering high-frequency nouns, verbs, pronouns, and essential grammatical particles with Mandarin pinyin and English glosses.
- [Wiktionary Classical Chinese Index](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:Classical_Chinese_language) - An open lexical reference providing character etymologies, Middle and Old Chinese reconstructed pronunciations, and citation sentences from antiquity.

## Grammar

- [Let's Learn: Classical Chinese (Colin Gorrie / YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLmp7RhkyEu0vihKsyv1xN8RZykaoe0Olb) - A step-by-step video lecture series working through J.J. Brandt's classical textbook to break down sentence patterns, syntax, and particles.
- [Wenyanwen Primer by Robert Eno (Indiana University ScholarWorks)](https://hdl.handle.net/2022/23480) - A complete, two-volume open-access university primer covering grammar rules, syntax notes, and graded Pre-Qin and Han reading passages.
- [Classical Chinese Grammar Notes on Wikibooks](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Classical_Chinese) - A community-maintained reference guide outlining classical word order, nominal and verbal predicates, and common grammatical function words (*xuzi*).
- [Outline of Classical Chinese Grammar by Edwin G. Pulleyblank (Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/outlineofclassic0000pull) - The standard linguistic reference grammar detailing nominal sentence structures, verbal aspect, negation patterns, and modal particles.
- [Introduction to Literary Chinese by J.J. Brandt (Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/introductiontoli00branuoft) - A classic public-domain textbook providing systematic graduated grammar lessons, vocabulary tables, and illustrative readings from ancient chronicles.
- [A First Course in Literary Chinese by Harold Shadick (Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/firstcourseinlit0000shad) - A renowned university primer featuring an extensive grammatical outline, structural analysis exercises, and unedited reading selections.

## Reading & Comprehensible Input

- [Chinese Text Project (ctext.org)](https://ctext.org/) - The premier open-access digital repository of pre-modern Chinese texts, featuring parallel English translations, full-text search, and interlinear character lookups.
- [An Introduction to Literary Chinese by Michael A. Fuller (Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/anintroductionto0000full) - A widely adopted intermediate reader featuring unedited philosophical and historical texts accompanied by running glosses and syntactic analysis.
- [The Library of Chinese Humanities (Harvard University / De Gruyter)](https://loch.hsites.harvard.edu/) - An open-access bilingual series providing facing-page translations of major classical literature, complete poetry corpora, and critical annotations.
- [Chinese Philosophical Texts by Mark Edward Lewis (Stanford University)](https://history.stanford.edu/publications/chinese-philosophical-texts) - An open-access guided reader containing unedited philosophical selections from *Mencius*, *Zhuangzi*, and *Han Feizi* with running character glosses.
- [Gushiwen / 古诗文网](https://www.gushiwen.cn/) - A comprehensive collection of classical poetry, parallel prose, and ancient essays featuring modern vernacular translations, annotations, and background commentaries.
- [Chinese Notes Digital Library & Text Reader](https://chinesenotes.com/) - An extensive classical library featuring an interactive mouseover reader with immediate vocabulary lookups and grammatical explanations.
- [Wikisource Classical Chinese / 維基文庫](https://zh.wikisource.org/) - A vast, community-curated archive of public-domain Chinese historical chronicles, philosophical anthologies, and literary works in traditional characters.

## Writing & Composition

- [Arch Chinese Character Worksheets Generator](https://www.archchinese.com/chinese_english_dictionary.html) - A customizable worksheet tool for practicing stroke order and handwriting for characters commonly found in classical prose.
- [Purple Culture Chinese Practice Sheet Generator](https://www.purpleculture.net/chinese_practice_sheet/) - A generator for creating printable practice grids (*tianzige* and *mizige*) to copy and transcribe classical passages stroke by stroke.
- [MARKUS: Text Analysis and Reading Platform (Leiden University)](https://dh.chinese-empires.eu/markus/) - A digital humanities platform designed to parse classical texts, identify named entities, and assist in structural segmentation and annotation.
- [Zdic Radical and Stroke Search](https://www.zdic.net/) - A crucial reference for checking historical glyph variants, traditional orthography, and seal script forms during text transcription.

## Cultural & Historical Context

- [Classics of Chinese Humanities: Guided Readings (Coursera / CUHK)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/classics-chinese-humanities) - A free-to-audit lecture course from The Chinese University of Hong Kong with guided readings of foundational texts, poetry, and historiography.
- [China Humanities: The Individual in Chinese Culture (Harvard Online / edX)](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/china-humanities-individual-chinese-culture) - A free-to-audit course exploring self-cultivation, poetry, historiography, and intellectual traditions taught by leading Harvard Sinologists.
- [China’s Political and Intellectual Foundations (Harvard Online / edX)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/chinas-political-and-intellectual-foundations-sage-kings-confucius) - A course examining the Warring States period, the Hundred Schools of Thought, and early statecraft that shaped classical writing.
- [New Horizons in Chinese Philosophy (edX / Tsinghua University)](https://www.edx.org/learn/philosophy/tsinghua-university-new-horizons-in-chinese-philosophy-zhong-guo-zhe-xue-xin-shi-ye) - A free-to-audit course exploring early Chinese intellectual debates, core philosophical terms, and their conceptual developments.
- [Sources of Chinese Tradition (Internet Archive)](https://archive.org/details/sourcesofchinese0001unse) - A landmark sourcebook providing primary texts in translation alongside introductions to pre-modern Chinese society, religion, and thought.
- [A Primer in Chinese Buddhist Writings by John Kieschnick (Stanford University)](https://religiousstudies.stanford.edu/primer-chinese-buddhist-writings) - An open courseware reader covering the history, translation dynamics, and distinctive linguistic register of medieval Chinese Buddhist prose.
