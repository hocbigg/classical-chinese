# Advanced Topics in Classical Chinese

This advanced curriculum is designed for students and researchers who have mastered the core grammar, syntax, and canon of Classical Chinese. It offers specialized pathways into primary source research, linguistic reconstruction, manuscript studies, philological criticism, and computational methods.

### Specialization Tracks Index

- [Paleography, Epigraphy, and Early Excavated Texts](#paleography-epigraphy-and-early-excavated-texts): Deciphers untransmitted primary records on oracle bones, bronze vessels, and bamboo/silk manuscripts from the Shang through Han dynasties.
- [Historical Phonology and Sinological Linguistics](#historical-phonology-and-sinological-linguistics): Investigates the phonetic systems, archaic rhyming patterns, and syntactic evolution from Old Chinese through Middle Chinese.
- [Textual Criticism, Commentarial Traditions, and Philology](#textual-criticism-commentarial-traditions-and-philology): Teaches traditional and modern methods of collation, textual stratification, Qing evidential scholarship, and canonical commentaries.
- [Medieval Hybrid Registers, Buddhist Chinese, and the Sinosphere](#medieval-hybrid-registers-buddhist-chinese-and-the-sinosphere): Analyzes non-standard registers, Buddhist translation idioms, Dunhuang vernacular manuscripts, and Literary Chinese across East Asia.
- [Digital Sinology, Computational Philology, and Spatial Analysis](#digital-sinology-computational-philology-and-spatial-analysis): Applies computational text mining, stylometry, prosopographical databases, and historical GIS to classical textual corpora.
