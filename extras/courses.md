# Great Courses in Classical Chinese

This curated directory features standout university OpenCourseWare, recorded lecture series, and high-production MOOCs in Classical Chinese language, literature, philosophy, and Sinological research methods.

## Classical Text Masterclasses & Guided Readings

- [Classics of Chinese Humanities: Guided Readings (Coursera / The Chinese University of Hong Kong / Leo Ou-fan Lee)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/classics-chinese-humanities)
- [China Humanities: The Individual in Chinese Culture (Harvard Online & edX / Harvard University / Peter K. Bol, Stephen Owen, Xiaofei Tian, Wai-yee Li, Michael Puett)](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/china-humanities-individual-chinese-culture)
- [It's Time to Read Zhuangzi (NTU OpenCourseWare / National Taiwan University / Pi-ming Tsai)](https://ocw.aca.ntu.edu.tw/ntu-ocw/ocw/cou/103S123)
- Methods in Sinology: Early Chinese Texts, Philology, and Manuscripts (YouTube / In Conversation with China / Boqun Zhou, Lukáš Zádrapa, Imre Galambos et al.) - search "Methods in Sinology" on YouTube

## Intellectual History & Classical Philosophy

- [China’s Political and Intellectual Foundations: From Sage Kings to Confucius and the Legalists (Harvard Online & edX / Harvard University / Peter K. Bol & William C. Kirby)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/chinas-political-and-intellectual-foundations-sage-kings-confucius)
- [Literati China: Examinations, Neo-Confucianism, and Later Imperial China (Harvard Online & edX / Harvard University / Peter K. Bol)](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/literati-china-examinations-neo-confucianism-and-later-imperial-china)
- Humanity and Nature in Chinese Thought (edX / The University of Hong Kong / Chad Hansen) - search "Humanity and Nature in Chinese Thought" on edX
- East Asian Confucianisms: Mencius (Coursera / National Taiwan University / Chun-chieh Huang) - search "East Asian Confucianisms: Mencius" on Coursera

## Poetics, Literary Culture & Aesthetic Forms

- [Tang Poetry: New Perspectives (Coursera / National Taiwan University / Li-chuan Ou)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/tang-poetry)
- [Chinese Garden Literature (Coursera / National Taiwan University / Shu-chuan Tsao)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/chinese-garden-literature-1)
