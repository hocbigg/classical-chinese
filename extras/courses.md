# Great Courses in Classical Chinese

This curated directory features standout university OpenCourseWare, recorded lecture series, and high-production MOOCs in Classical Chinese language, literature, philosophy, and Sinological research methods.

## Classical Text Readings & Foundational Masterclasses

- [Classics of Chinese Humanities: Guided Readings (Coursera / The Chinese University of Hong Kong / Leo Ou-fan Lee)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/classics-chinese-humanities)
- [China Humanities: The Individual in Chinese Culture (edX / Harvard University / Peter K. Bol, Stephen Owen, Xiaofei Tian, Wai-yee Li, Michael Puett)](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/china-humanities-individual-chinese-culture)
- [Zhuangzi: Reading Early Daoist Classics (NTU OpenCourseWare / National Taiwan University / Pi-fang Tsai)](http://ocw.aca.ntu.edu.tw/)
- [Library of Chinese Humanities Text & Audio Open Series (Library of Chinese Humanities / Stephen Owen et al.)](https://libraryofchinesehumanities.org/)

## Intellectual History & Classical Philosophy

- [China’s Political and Intellectual Foundations: From Sage Kings to Confucius and the Legalists (edX / HarvardX / Peter K. Bol & William C. Kirby)](https://www.edx.org/school/harvardx)
- [Humanity and Nature in Chinese Thought (edX / The University of Hong Kong / Chad Hansen)](https://www.edx.org/)
- [Literati China: Examinations, Neo-Confucianism, and Later Imperial China (edX / HarvardX / Peter K. Bol)](https://www.edx.org/school/harvardx)
- [East Asian Confucianisms: Philosophical Texts and Humanism (Coursera / National Taiwan University / Chun-chieh Huang)](https://www.coursera.org/)

## Poetics, Literary Culture & Aesthetic Forms

- [Classics of Chinese Literature (MIT OpenCourseWare / Emma J. Teng)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21g-044-classics-of-chinese-literature-fall-2011/)
- [Chinese Garden Literature and Literati Aesthetics (Coursera / National Taiwan University / Shu-chuan Tsao)](https://www.coursera.org/)
- [The World of Tang Poetry and Classical Verse (Coursera / Peking University)](https://www.coursera.org/)

## Historiography, Commentary & Sinological Methods

- [Records of the Grand Historian: Sima Qian's Shiji (NTU OpenCourseWare / National Taiwan University / Shih-hao Lu)](http://ocw.aca.ntu.edu.tw/)
- [Digital Methods and Corpus Analysis in Pre-Modern Chinese (Chinese Text Project / Donald Sturgeon)](https://ctext.org/tools)
- [China Biographical Database and Historical GIS Spatial Methods (Harvard University / Center for Geographic Analysis & Harvard-Yenching Institute)](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/cbdb)
